Profit vs. cash

A business owner may know how much revenue the company generates without fully understanding where the profit goes.

Profitable on paper
But where is the cash?

Your business can report a profit while having significantly less cash available. Understanding the difference helps you plan expenses, investments, and future growth.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need complete financial statements to get started?

Not necessarily. We can start by reviewing the information you already have and identifying what may be missing before conducting a deeper financial analysis.

How is financial advisory different from accounting?

Accounting focuses on maintaining and reporting financial information. Our advisory services help business owners interpret that information, assess financial performance, and make informed management decisions.

Can you work with my existing accountant?

Absolutely. We can work alongside your accounting team to review financial information, analyse performance, and provide additional financial insights for management.

Do you work with foreign business owners in Bali?

Yes. We work with international founders and business owners operating in Bali and Indonesia. Consultations can be conducted in English or Indonesian.

Can we work together remotely?

Yes. Our advisory process can be conducted online, depending on the scope and needs of your business.

What happens during the initial consultation?

We discuss your business, current financial concerns, and the type of support you may need. The initial consultation is separate from the paid Financial Health Review.

How much does a Financial Health Review cost?

The fee depends on the scope of the review and the financial information involved. We will explain the proposed scope and pricing before you decide to proceed.
